How the counters work

The Death Counter shows the latest complete annual total. Daily and Monthly are averages calculated from that annual total. They are not claims that a specific death occurred at a particular second.

Data source

The CDC/NCHS reports 48,824 U.S. suicide deaths in 2024 and an age-adjusted rate of 13.7 per 100,000. The CDC also provides monthly and yearly suicide data through its data systems.
